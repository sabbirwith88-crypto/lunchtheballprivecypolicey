Lunch The Ball

This Privacy Policy explains how Lunch The Ball (“Game”, “we”, “us”, or “our”), published by mdalamin97, handles information when you install or use the Game.

1. About the Game

Lunch The Ball is a mobile ball-launcher game. The Game does not require users to create an account or sign in. Gameplay can be used without providing us with a name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or messages.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The Game stores certain gameplay and preference information locally on your device so that the Game can remember your progress and settings. This may include:

  • Coins and other in-game progress;
  • Unlocked and selected ball skins and launcher skins;
  • Achievements and daily reward status;
  • Level, score, or similar progress information;
  • Other non-account gameplay state needed to operate the Game.

This information is stored locally by the Game and is not an online user account. It is not sent to us. If you uninstall the Game or clear its application data, locally stored gameplay information may be removed by Android.

3. Advertising and Google AdMob

The Game uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads to display advertisements. The Game may show interstitial advertisements and optional rewarded advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may process information associated with the device or advertising service in order to provide, measure, protect, and improve advertising services. Depending on the device, region, consent status, and applicable settings, this may include information such as:

  • Advertising identifier or other advertising-related identifiers;
  • IP address and approximate location inferred from an IP address;
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system, app version, and technical information;
  • Information about advertisements that are shown, viewed, clicked, or otherwise interacted with;
  • Diagnostics and performance information, and information used for fraud prevention, security, measurement, and advertising delivery.

We do not independently sell your personal information to advertisers. Information processed by Google or other advertising partners is subject to their own privacy policies and applicable controls.

4. Rewarded Advertisements

The Game may offer optional in-game rewards, such as coins, after you choose to watch a rewarded advertisement.

Watching a rewarded advertisement is optional. The Game grants the associated reward after the advertising system reports the required completion or reward event.

5. Advertising Choices and Consent

Depending on your location and applicable law (for example, in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the Game shows a privacy and consent choice for advertising using Google's User Messaging Platform before personalized advertising is served. Advertising personalization may also be affected by your Android or Google account/device settings.

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, from your Android settings (Settings → Privacy or Google → Ads). You can also manage Google ad settings at https://adssettings.google.com.

6. Children and Family Privacy

Lunch The Ball is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum age of digital consent in your country). We do not intentionally ask anyone to provide direct personal information such as name, email address, phone number, contacts, photographs, or messages, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Parents or guardians who have questions about privacy or believe that information has been provided to us can contact us using the email address in Section 15.

7. Third-Party Services

The Game uses third-party services for advertising and related technical functions. The primary advertising service used by the Game is Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads.

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing the relevant provider's privacy documentation:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

8. Internet and Network Access, Permissions

The Game uses internet and network-state access to request and display advertisements and to support services provided by Google. Network access is not required for the Game to store local gameplay progress, although advertisements will not load without an internet connection.

The Game also uses the following permissions:

  • VIBRATE: to provide vibration feedback during gameplay;
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID) and Android ad services permissions: so that Google's advertising components can read the advertising identifier and use Android's privacy-preserving advertising features;
  • WAKE_LOCK and FOREGROUND_SERVICE: included by Google's advertising components to run ad-related tasks reliably. The Game does not use them to track you.

9. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect Directly

Through the Game itself, we do not intentionally request or collect:

  • Contacts or address books;
  • SMS or call history;
  • Camera or microphone recordings;
  • User photos or personal files;
  • Precise GPS location;
  • Passwords or payment-card information (the Game has no real-money purchases; the in-game shop uses only coins earned in the Game);
  • A personal user account profile.

This section describes the Game's direct behavior. Third-party services, including advertising services, may independently process technical or advertising-related information as described above.

10. Data Sharing

We do not operate our own servers for the Game, do not maintain a user account database, and do not intentionally sell user information. Information may be processed by third-party service providers when necessary to provide services used by the Game, particularly advertising, security, fraud prevention, measurement, and related technical functions.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay information saved locally by the Game remains on your device until it is removed by the Game, cleared through Android's app settings, or removed when the Game is uninstalled, subject to Android's storage behavior.

We do not maintain a separate online account for players, so there is no player account database that you need to request us to delete. Information handled by Google or another third-party provider is retained and deleted according to that provider's policies and applicable law.

12. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to avoid unnecessary collection of personal information through the Game. Advertising-related traffic between your device and Google is encrypted in transit. However, no app, device, internet transmission, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning personal information, such as rights to access, delete, correct, restrict, or object to certain processing, or to withdraw consent. The availability and scope of these rights depend on applicable law and the party processing the information.

For information processed by Google through advertising services, you may need to use Google's privacy and advertising controls or contact Google directly. For locally stored gameplay data, you can generally remove it by clearing the Game's app data or uninstalling the Game.
